# G1 independently signed review-floor publication protocol (reference)

**21 November 2026 — protocol-only; no independent service deployed.** The design closes the gap between local `REVIEW_APPROVAL_CONSUMED_ONLY` and publication of an authenticated monotonic register floor. It does not create the independent trust domain or production replay fencing.

The new `src/security/provider-witness-recovery-floor-publication.mjs` includes canonical, domain-separated `PUBLISH_REVIEW_ONLY_FLOOR` proposals requiring two distinct independently configured Ed25519 custodian signatures. Each signed proposal binds issuer and audience, a unique publication ID, exact old/new `{serial,headHash}`, the consumed approval ID and evidence digest, and an ISO validity window no longer than 24 hours. The new serial must be exactly old serial+1. No statement can authorize system recovery.

The verifier checks the **whole canonical consumption ledger**, the latest consumption record, the preceding record’s exact externally pinned head, and matching last approval/evidence IDs. Signatures over a future head without a corresponding complete ledger are insufficient.

The `openReferenceRecoveryFloorPublicationGate` wraps an injected authority with signed `read` receipts and a required atomic `compareAndSwap`; its signed reads use a separately pinned authority public key. Opening requires the authority's authenticated head to match the separately supplied trusted floor twice. Publication checks both custodian signatures and ledger evidence, then calls remote compare-and-swap with a bounded timeout. Successful acknowledgement requires a valid signed receipt AND a matching fresh remote read. A timeout, lost acknowledgement, stale authority state or remote read mismatch permanently faults the gate. No retries or silent trusted-floor promotion happen after an uncertain commit.

**Adversarial CI** uses an in-memory, atomic-style authority and synthetic keys for 11 tests including stale/forked evidence, forged or duplicate custodians, time/scope rejection, stale-floor restart, 16 concurrent calls, two concurrent gate instances, lost ACK, late remote completion, authority outage and safe prewrite rejection. These tests do not establish actual remote durability, quorum, independent key custody, real-world network partitions, external counter publication, disk-fsync durability or replay security under total privileged compromise.

**Mandatory production contract:** the independent authority must authenticate the publisher, independently verify signed proposals and full ledger evidence, atomically fence concurrent custodians, durably store its monotonic signed head, maintain an independently administered high-water mark against replay/rollback, and expose authenticated reads with recovery and revocation procedures. A successfully published review floor does **not** authorize recovery, delete locks, or resume processing. PR #4 remains draft, unmerged, undeployed.

## Signed receipt v2 challenge and expiry (21 November 2026)

The authority read and CAS acknowledgement contract now requires a fresh cryptographically random, canonical 32-byte base64url **request nonce**. The authority signs that exact request nonce with issuer, audience, serial, head hash, issuedAt and expiresAt under the v2 receipt domain. v1 receipts and nonce-less callers fail closed; this is an intentional **breaking change**, not backward-compatible migration. The gate uses a new random nonce for each startup read, audit read, pre-publication read, CAS acknowledgement and post-commit read. It validates the expected challenge and a bounded signed validity window (default maximum age 10 seconds; absolute signed window ceiling 30 seconds), including expiry and future-issued rejection.

An old authentic signed head cannot satisfy a newly challenged request even when the head hash has not changed. Nonce binding and receipt age **do not prove** that the authority uses an independently trusted clock, that its signing key or high-water mark is outside host control, or that CAS is durably linearizable across machines. The test double remains an in-process fixture, and no remote security certification is implied. Independent key custody, authority transport authentication, clock discipline, revocation and distributed fencing remain P0 production gates.
